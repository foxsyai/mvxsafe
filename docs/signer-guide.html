Approving an action at mvxsafe.io

A one-page guide for board members of a multisig safe

The tokens in a safe are something no single person can move. Somebody proposes an action and the other board members read it. Those who agree sign. Nothing happens until enough of them have signed: the quorum, for example two of three.

Your part takes about a minute. It is the same every time.

1

Open the safe and connect your wallet

Go to mvxsafe.io, open the safe you were asked to sign for and press Connect.

  • Choose how you sign: xPortal (scan the code with your phone), the MultiversX browser extension, a Ledger or the web wallet.
  • Connecting only proves who you are, it cannot move anything.
2

Read what you are approving

The pending action is written in plain words, for example: Send 1,000 USDC-c76f1f to erd1qyu5wthldzr8wx5c9ucg8kjagg0jfs53s8nr3zpz3hypefsdd8ssycr6th.

  • Check the amount, the token and the whole address against what you were told, not only its first and last letters.
  • If anything is different, do not sign. Close the page and ask the person who proposed it.
3

Sign

Press Sign, confirm in your wallet and you are done.

  • Your signature counts towards the quorum.
  • It costs a tiny network fee, so keep a little EGLD in your wallet, around 0.05 is plenty.
4

Optional: carry it out

If your signature completes the quorum, a Carry it out button appears. Pressing it is what actually moves the tokens.

  • You do not have to. Any board member can press it later with the same result.
  • If you prefer to stop after signing, simply close the page and tell the others.
Three things that are always true. Your keys never leave your wallet: mvxsafe.io never sees them. Signing approves one specific action and nothing else. It can be undone while the action is still waiting. Everything is recorded publicly on the MultiversX blockchain, so any signature can be checked afterwards.
mvxsafe.io is open source: github.com/foxsyai/mvxsafe. It never holds keys. Any copy of it shows the same safe, as does any other interface for the same contract.
