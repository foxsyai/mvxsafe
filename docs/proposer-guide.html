Proposing an action at mvxsafe.io

A one-page guide for whoever asks a safe to move funds

Nothing leaves a safe until enough board members have signed. Proposing puts a request in front of them. On its own it moves nothing.

You need a wallet that sits on the safe's board or is one of its proposers. You also need a little EGLD for fees: around 0.05 is plenty.

1

Open the safe and connect your wallet

Go to mvxsafe.io, open the safe and press Connect.

  • Choose how you sign: xPortal (scan the code with your phone), the MultiversX browser extension, a Ledger, the web wallet or MetaMask on a computer.
  • If the safe is not in your list yet, paste its address under Add a safe.
2

Fill in the proposal

Under Propose an action, choose Send tokens. For EGLD, the network coin, choose Send EGLD.

  • Token: check its full code, not only its name. A token with another code is another token.
  • Recipient: paste the address and check it twice, character by character, against where it came from.
  • Amount: start with a small test amount when the address is new to you. Send the rest once the test has arrived.

Press Propose and confirm in your wallet. A board member who proposes has signed by doing so. A proposer has not, as proposers never sign.

3

Tell the others

Tell the board members who still have to sign what you proposed, by phone or message: the token, the amount and the whole address. They read it on their own screen and sign. The signer's guide shows them how.

4

Carry it out

Once the signatures reach the quorum, a Carry it out button appears. Pressing it is what actually moves the funds: nothing moves before it.

  • Any board member or proposer can press it. Leave the gas figure as it is.
  • The action then joins the safe's history, linked to the explorer. Check that it arrived before you send more.
Changed your mind? While the action waits, Remove my signature takes your approval back. Once no signature is left on it, Discard throws it away. Changes to the board, the quorum and contract calls follow the same steps: the full guide at mvxsafe.io/guide covers them.
mvxsafe.io is open source: github.com/foxsyai/mvxsafe. It never holds keys. Any copy of it shows the same safe, as does any other interface for the same contract.
