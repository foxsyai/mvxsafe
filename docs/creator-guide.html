Creating a safe at mvxsafe.io

A one-page guide for whoever sets up a multisig safe

A safe holds funds that no single person can move. You choose its board and how many members have to sign before anything moves: the quorum, for example two of three. You do this once.

You need every board member's address, each starting with erd1. You also need about 0.1 EGLD in your own wallet.

1

Connect your wallet

Go to mvxsafe.io, press Connect and choose how you sign: xPortal, the MultiversX browser extension, a Ledger or the web wallet. Connecting only proves who you are, it cannot move anything.

2

Fill in the create page

Press Create a safe on the main page and give the safe a name, kept in your browser only.

  • Under Board, your address fills the first row. Paste the others below; Add a member adds a row. Compare every address with its source: a wrong one is a seat nobody can ever use.
  • Under Signatures needed, choose more than one and fewer than the whole board, for example two of three. For a board of two or more, the page warns you otherwise.
3

Sign the two transactions

Press the button, which names your choice: Create a 2 of 3 safe, for example. Your wallet shows two transactions. The first creates the safe, the second hands it to itself. From then on everything it does needs the quorum, its code included: with two or more signatures needed, nobody, you included, can move its funds or change it alone. The fee is about 0.073 EGLD.

4

Check it

The page opens the new safe. Check the quorum and every address under Board and proposers.

  • In the MultiversX Explorer, the safe's Owner line must show the same address as its Address line: the safe owns itself.
  • If a red warning sits at the top of the safe's page, stop and send it no funds.
5

Share it

Copy the address with the icon next to it and send it to the board: each member adds it once under Add a safe. Anyone can send funds to it. About 0.01 EGLD in it lets the network try a token payment before anyone signs; the safe itself never pays fees.

Worth knowing. This site keeps no special access to the safe and never sees your keys. A change to the board needs the quorum too: if the board loses more wallets than it can spare, nothing can move again, so replace a lost wallet early.
mvxsafe.io is open source: github.com/foxsyai/mvxsafe. It never holds keys. Any copy of it shows the same safe, as does any other interface for the same contract.
